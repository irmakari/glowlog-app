GlowLog helps you track skincare products, routines, treatments, water intake, and progress. The app stores the information you enter in a database on your device. Product photos you select are copied into the app's local storage. GlowLog does not currently create an account or send this information to a GlowLog server.

If you add treatments, such as prescribed medications or creams, GlowLog stores their names, the dose notes you type, their schedule, and which doses you marked as taken or skipped. This is health information. Like the rest of your data, it stays in the database on your device. GlowLog uses it only to show your doses, schedule your reminders, and flag skincare products that may not mix well with a treatment. Those warnings are general information, not medical advice.

GlowLog asks for access to selected photos when you add a product photo. It asks for notification permission only if you turn on routine or treatment reminders. Treatment reminders show the treatment name and your dose note on your lock screen, depending on your iOS notification settings. You can change these permissions in iOS Settings.

You can export a JSON backup from the app and share it using the system share sheet. Once you share a backup, the destination you choose handles that copy under its own terms. The current JSON backup contains text data, including any treatments and dose history, and photo file references, but not the photo files themselves. Because a backup can include health information, share it only with places you trust. Imported backups replace the data already stored in the app.

You can delete app data by removing the app from your device. GlowLog does not currently provide cloud sync or an online account recovery service.
