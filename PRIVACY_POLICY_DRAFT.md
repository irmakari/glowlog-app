GlowLog helps you track skincare products, routines, water intake, and progress. The app stores the information you enter in a database on your device. Product photos you select are copied into the app's local storage. GlowLog does not currently create an account or send this information to a GlowLog server.

GlowLog asks for access to selected photos when you add a product photo. It asks for notification permission only if you turn on routine reminders. You can change these permissions in iOS Settings.

You can export a JSON backup from the app and share it using the system share sheet. Once you share a backup, the destination you choose handles that copy under its own terms. The current JSON backup contains text data and photo file references, but not the photo files themselves. Imported backups replace the data already stored in the app.

You can delete app data by removing the app from your device. GlowLog does not currently provide cloud sync or an online account recovery service.
